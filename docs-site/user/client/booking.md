# Booking and paying

You need an account to book. Sign in with your phone number and the code you receive.

## Booking

1. Choose a service, a day and a time (or dates, for resorts).
2. The app shows a **quote**: the price, any discount and whether it is available.
3. Confirm and choose how to pay.

You can only book a slot that is in the future, has space, and is not closed. You cannot book a center that has blocked you.

## Paying

| Method | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Online** | You pay through the payment provider. The booking stays **pending** until payment succeeds, then becomes **confirmed**. |
| **Cash on arrival** | Available only when the center allows it. The booking is confirmed immediately. |

If you do not pay online within the hold time, the booking is cancelled and the slot is released. If a payment still arrives after that, it is **refunded automatically**.

## One checkout, one center

You can pay for several bookings together, but only from the **same center**.

## My bookings

**My bookings** is one list of your reservations, packages and memberships, newest first. Unpaid packages and memberships are not listed.

## Cancelling

You can cancel until the cutoff (a number of hours before the start time).

- Online payments are **refunded**. If the refund cannot be made, the booking stays.
- Cash or unpaid bookings have nothing to refund.
- A session booked with a package is given back to the package.

## Changing the time

Ask the center to **reschedule**. The price must stay the same.
