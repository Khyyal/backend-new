# Browsing centers and services

You do not need an account to browse.

## Home

The home screen shows banners, recommended centers and services, running offers and campaigns, and the most popular or nearest centers. Allow location access to see centers nearest to you first.

## Centers

Open a center to see its description, address, photos, tags, contact phone, distance from you, and an overview of its services.

Only centers that are **visible and active** appear.

## Services

Open a service to see its details, price options and, for riding, events and training, **available times** for today and the next 14 days.

## Prices

- The **starting price** is the lowest option ("from").
- While an offer is running you see the **original price, the discounted price and a badge** such as "-15%".
- Offers with a minimum spend or on the whole order are applied when you check out.
- For resorts, extras are not part of the starting price.

## Language

Names and descriptions are shown in the language of your app (English or Arabic).
