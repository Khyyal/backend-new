# User guide

This guide explains how to use the platform. Pick the part that matches your role.

| You are | Read |
|---|---|
| **Platform staff** (system user) managing centers, promotions, the homepage, fees and payouts | [Admin panel](./admin/getting-around) |
| **A center owner or team member** running services, reservations and payouts | [Center dashboard](./center/overview) |
| **A client** browsing, booking and paying in the app | [Client app](./client/browsing) |

Developers should read the [Developer guide](/dev/) instead.

## Amounts and dates

- All amounts are in **SAR** and are shown to apps in **halalas** (1 SAR = 100 halalas). `15000` means 150.00 SAR.
- Times are **UTC**. There is no per-center time zone.
- Names and descriptions exist in **English and Arabic**. Apps send `Accept-Language` to choose.
