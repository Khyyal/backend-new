# Promotions

You need the **promotions** permission. Promotions give clients a discount on your services only.

## Creating one

**Promotions → New.** It starts as a **draft**. Choose:

- the discount: **percentage**, **fixed amount** or **fixed price**
- an optional **maximum discount** and **minimum spend**
- the **dates** and an optional **weekly schedule**
- which services it **targets**
- whether it needs a **coupon code**

Then **Activate**. You can **pause** and resume, or **archive** it. Centers pay for their own promotions; the platform can run its own separately.

Once a promotion has been used, its discount and targets are locked. Archive it and create a new one to change them.

## Coupons

Add coupons one by one, or **generate** a batch. Codes are saved in capitals. Set a total limit and a per-client limit.

## What clients see

While an automatic promotion is running, your service shows the **original** and **discounted** starting price with a badge such as "-15%". Promotions with a minimum spend, or that apply to the whole order, are applied at checkout and not shown on the service.

## Results

Open **Redemptions** to see where a promotion was used.
