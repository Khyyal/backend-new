# Center dashboard overview

The dashboard is where a center runs its business: services, reservations, purchases, promotions, team and payouts.

## Getting in

1. Sign in with your phone number and the one-time code.
2. Pick your center. The **owner** (primary user) is asked for their password again before entering.

You can only use the dashboard when the platform has set your center to **Active**. A **Pending** center is waiting for review; a **Blocked** center cannot operate.

## What you see depends on your permissions

The owner can do everything. Other team members only see what their permissions allow, for example managing one service type, viewing purchases, or handling reservations. See [Team and permissions](./team).

## Going live

**Profile completion** lists what is missing (for example description, images, services or your verified email). Set your center **Visible** when you want clients to find it.

## Privacy policy and terms

The center app links to the privacy policy and terms and conditions for centers. They open inside the app in your language.

## Main areas

| Area | Page |
|---|---|
| Services, trainers and prices | [Managing services](./services) |
| Opening hours, closures and blocks | [Schedules, periods and blocks](./schedules-blocks) |
| Bookings | [Reservations](./reservations) |
| Money | [Purchases, balance and payouts](./purchases-payouts) |
| Discounts | [Promotions](./promotions) |
| Staff | [Team and permissions](./team) |

**Plans and subscription:** browse the plans, subscribe, and see your current subscription. Only the owner can cancel. Subscriptions last one billing period and have no VAT or invoices.

Also: **Analytics** (your sales and bookings), **Ratings**, **Blocked clients**, **Clients** and **Notifications**. You can export purchases and clients if you have the export permission.

## Verify your email

Add a contact email to your center, then ask for a verification code: we email you a 6-digit code that is valid for 10 minutes. Enter it to confirm the email. Only the primary user can do this. If you change the email later, you need to verify it again.
