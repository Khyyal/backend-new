# Horses

Keep a list of the horses at your center under **Horses**. You need the *Manage horses* permission.

Each horse has:

- a **name**
- a **room** number (several horses can share a room)
- a **photo** (optional)
- an **owner**: either your center, or one of your clients

## Adding a horse

Click **New**, enter the name and room, and add a photo if you want. To record a client's horse, pick the client as the owner. If that client isn't in your client list yet, they're added to it. Leave the owner empty for a horse your center owns.

## Finding and editing horses

Filter the list to show only your center's horses or only clients' horses, show the horses of one client, or search by name. When you edit a horse you can change its name, room, photo or owner. Clearing the owner makes the horse your center's.

## Removing a horse

Deleting a horse removes it from the list.
