# Team and permissions

You need the **users** permission to manage the team.

## Roles

- The **owner** (primary user) can do everything, cannot be changed or removed, and must re-enter their password to open the dashboard.
- Everyone else is a **team member** with specific permissions.

## Adding someone

**Users → New.** Enter their phone number and tick their permissions.

- You can only give permissions you have yourself.
- You cannot change your own permissions.

## Permissions

| Group | Examples |
|---|---|
| Services | Manage riding, visit, event, resort, horse care, horse service, training or membership services (one permission each) |
| Purchases | View purchases for each service type; see prices; see other trainers' sales; see sales without a trainer |
| Reservations | Manage reservations; edit reservations |
| Clients | Manage blocked clients; export clients |
| Business | Promotions; analytics; ratings; payouts; export purchases; manage users |

## Removing someone

Removing a team member signs them out of the dashboard immediately. If they belong to no other center, their account is removed.
