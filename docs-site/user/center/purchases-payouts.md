# Purchases, balance and payouts

## Purchases

**Purchases** shows what clients bought from you. Which ones you see depends on your permissions: you need the view permission for each service type, and separate permissions to see other trainers' sales, sales not linked to a trainer, and prices. The owner sees everything. You can export purchases if you have the permission. Every purchase has a 6-character **code** you can look it up by.

## Fees

The platform charges a **fee** on each sale. It is taken from your earnings and does **not** change what the client pays. It is returned if the sale is cancelled or fully refunded.

## Balance

**Balance** shows what the platform owes you.

- **Online payments** are added after a short hold period.
- Fees, refunds and discounts paid for by the platform adjust the balance.
- **Cash and manual payments** you collected yourself are not part of it.

## Payouts

Every week the platform creates a **payout** for your available balance and pays it to your bank account. You can see each payout and its status (**Pending**, **Paid**, **Cancelled**).

The **owner should set the payout account** (IBAN). A payout is still created without it, but the platform cannot transfer it until you add the account, and payouts already created keep the account they had at the time. A zero or negative balance is also carried over.

## Verifying a payment

If a client says they paid but the booking is still pending, use **Verify payment** on the purchase. The payment status is checked directly with the payment provider.
