# Reservations

**Reservations** lists every booking at your center. Filter by date, status or service, or use the **calendar**.

Every booking has a 6-character **code** (for example `K7QX2M`). A client can show it at the desk and you can look the booking up by it.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Booked, waiting for payment |
| **Confirmed** | Paid (or cash on arrival) |
| **Completed** | The visit happened |
| **No-show** | The client did not come |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled; the slot is free again |

Pending, confirmed, completed and no-show bookings all **use up capacity**. Only cancelled ones free it.

## What you can do

You need the reservations permission.

- **Create** a booking for a client. Use **quote** first to check the price and availability.
- **Find a client by phone** at the desk. You can find any client of the app, even one who has never booked with you. If nobody has that phone, **add the client** with their phone, first name and last name. They join your client list and can later sign in to the app with the same phone.
- **Pick the horse** a booking is for, from your center's horses (yours or your clients'), or remove it. You need the edit permission. This works whatever the booking's status.
- **Cancel** a booking.
- **Complete** or mark **no-show**, but only after the slot has started.
- **Reschedule** a pending or confirmed booking to another slot. The price must stay the same; if it would change, cancel and rebook.
  If the new slot is full you are told there are not enough places; confirm the move to book it anyway (this overbooks the slot). Other rules, such as opening hours, still apply.

## Stabling a horse

To board a client's horse, book a **stabling** stay from the horse. Choose the stabling service, a **monthly price** or the **price per day**, the start date, and any extras.

- A monthly price covers its months from the start date (one month from 1 December ends on 31 December).
- The price per day starts a stay with no end date. It lasts until you complete or cancel it, and the first day is charged.
- Only horses that belong to a client can be stabled. The client is the horse's owner.
- A horse can only have one stay at a time. Complete or cancel the current stay before booking an overlapping one.
- You can enter a **different total**. It is charged as you enter it, and offers and coupons are not applied.
- Mark the stay **paid** if the client already paid. Otherwise it is confirmed with the payment still to collect.
- A stabling stay cannot be rescheduled. Cancel it and book again.

## Cancelling and refunds

- Clients can cancel only until the cutoff set by the platform (a number of hours before the start). Staff are not bound by it.
- An **online** payment is refunded automatically. If the refund cannot be made, the cancellation does not go through.
- A **manual** payment is marked as needing a manual refund.
- **Cash** or unpaid bookings have nothing to refund.
- A cancelled booking from a package gives the session back.
- On a client's package you can book a session for them, add sessions, set or remove the trainer (training packages) and extend the expiry date. Extending an expired package that still has sessions makes it active again.
- You can **cancel a client's package** as long as no money is held for it (unpaid, cash, or already refunded). Its upcoming sessions are cancelled with it and the client is notified. A package that was paid for is cancelled by **refunding its purchase** instead, and a package that came with a membership cannot be cancelled on its own.
- You can also extend a client's membership expiry date; an expired membership becomes active again. The packages that came with it keep their own dates.
- Check a member in when they arrive; every check-in is added to the membership's log, which also lists the guests the member invited (with the visit created for each). Checking in does not change any booking, and only active memberships can be checked in.

## Unpaid bookings

An online booking that is not paid within the hold time is cancelled automatically. If the payment arrives afterwards, it is refunded automatically.

## Why a booking is refused

Common reasons: the slot is full or in the past, the service is inactive, the client is blocked by your center, a block or closure overlaps, or the service type's booking option is switched off.
