# Getting around the admin panel

The admin panel is for platform staff (**system users**). What you can see and do depends on the **role** you were given. If a section is missing or an action is refused, ask an administrator to check your role.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password. Inactive users cannot sign in.

## Legal pages

**Settings → Legal pages** holds the privacy policy and terms and conditions of the client app and of the center app, each in English and Arabic. Write them in the editor (headings, lists, links, alignment) and **Save**; both languages are required. The apps open the published page right away. Use **Open page** to see it as clients and centers do. You need the **Legal documents** permission (`legal_documents.manage`).

## Notifications

The bell shows platform events (for example a new center registering). Mark one or all as read. Allow browser notifications when the panel asks to also get them as pop-ups while the panel is closed or in the background; signing out stops them on that browser.

## Search

The search box in the top bar finds a record anywhere in the panel. Click it, or press **⌘K** (Mac) or **Ctrl+K**, and type at least two characters: a center name, phone or email; a client's name or phone; a service name; or the 6-character code of a reservation or purchase. Results are grouped into centers, clients, reservations, purchases, client packages, client memberships and services, with up to five per group. Pick one to open it. You only see the groups your role allows.

## Lists

Lists are paged and can be searched and filtered. Large lists can be exported to a spreadsheet; see [Analytics and exports](./analytics).

On the centers, clients, purchases, subscriptions, payouts and center ledger lists, a column header with arrows can be clicked to sort by it: once for ascending, again for descending, and a third time to go back to newest first. The sort stays when you change filters, and exports keep the same order.

## The documentation

Staff with the **API docs** permission can also open these guides and the technical endpoint reference. Use **Sign out** in the top bar when you are done.
