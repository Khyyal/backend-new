# Getting around the admin panel

The admin panel is for platform staff (**system users**). What you can see and do depends on the **role** you were given. If a section is missing or an action is refused, ask an administrator to check your role.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password. Inactive users cannot sign in.

## What you can manage

| Area | Page |
|---|---|
| Centers, their services, reservations and purchases | [Centers](./centers) |
| Client accounts | [Clients](./clients) |
| Discounts, campaigns and coupons | [Promotions](./promotions) |
| What clients see first in the app | [Homepage](./homepage) |
| Push and WhatsApp messages | [Notifications](./notifications) |
| Plans and center subscriptions | [Plans and subscriptions](./billing) |
| Fees, payments and center payouts | [Purchases, fees and payouts](./payments-payouts) |
| Dashboards and spreadsheets | [Analytics and exports](./analytics) |
| Staff accounts, roles, cities and tags | [Users and roles](./users-roles) |

## Notifications

The bell shows platform events (for example a new center registering). Mark one or all as read.

## Lists

Lists are paged and can be searched and filtered. Large lists can be exported to a spreadsheet; see [Analytics and exports](./analytics).

## The documentation

Staff with the **API docs** permission can also open these guides and the technical endpoint reference. Use **Sign out** in the top bar when you are done.
