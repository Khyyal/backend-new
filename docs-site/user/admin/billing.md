# Plans and subscriptions

Centers can subscribe to a **plan**. Plans are managed with the plan permissions; subscriptions with the **change subscriptions** permission.

## Plans

A plan has a name, price in SAR, a **billing interval** (monthly or yearly), optional **trial days**, features and limits, and a status (**Active** or **Inactive**). Centers can only subscribe to **active** plans.

Create and edit plans under **Plans**, switch a plan on or off, and attach **features** and **limits** to it. The catalog of features and limits itself needs the **manage catalog** permission.

## Subscriptions

**Subscriptions** lists every center's subscription. You can also change a center's subscription from its page. Centers can subscribe and cancel on their own from the dashboard (see [Center overview](../center/overview)).

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Trialing** | In a free trial |
| **Active** | Paid and running |
| **Pending** | Waiting for payment |
| **Canceled** | Stopped by the owner |
| **Expired** | Ran past its end date |

- A center cannot hold two active or trialing subscriptions to the same plan.
- A subscription lasts one interval from its start. **Renewing** starts a new one from the previous end date, with no trial.
- Only the center **owner** can cancel.
- There is no VAT, invoicing or proration in subscriptions.
- Unpaid subscriptions are released automatically.
