# Notification campaigns

Campaigns send a message to many clients or center users at once. You need the **notification campaigns** permission.

## Creating a campaign

1. **Audience:** **Clients** or **Centers**.
2. **Channel:** **Push** (in the app) or **WhatsApp**.
3. **Who:** **All**, or **Selected** (choose up to 5000 people).
4. **Content:**
   - Push needs a title and body in **both English and Arabic**.
   - WhatsApp uses an approved **template**; fill in every variable it has.
5. **When:** send now, or schedule.

## Lifecycle

**Draft → Scheduled → Sending → Sent** (or **Failed**). You can **cancel** a draft or scheduled campaign; a cancelled one cannot be restarted. **Duplicate** a campaign to reuse it.

- You can edit content and recipients only while it is a **draft** or **scheduled**.
- You cannot delete a campaign that is sending.
- Scheduled campaigns go out within a minute of their time.

## Checking delivery

Open **Deliveries** to see what happened per recipient. A retry never sends the same WhatsApp message twice.

## WhatsApp templates

Manage templates under **WhatsApp templates**. A template's variables must match what the campaign supplies.
