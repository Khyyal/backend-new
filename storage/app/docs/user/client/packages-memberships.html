Packages and memberships ​

Packages ​

A package is a bundle of sessions for one service, bought once at a lower price per session.

  • Buy it from the service. It is pending until payment succeeds, then active.
  • Packages are always paid online, never on arrival.
  • A center can pause a package. A paused package is not shown and cannot be bought, but packages you already own keep working.
  • Book a session from your package. It is confirmed immediately and costs nothing more. Each booking uses one session.
  • When the sessions run out the package is exhausted. It also expires after its validity period, counted from when it became active.
  • A package linked to a trainer can only be used with that trainer.
  • You can give a package a knight name of your own and change it any time. The center can mark a package as controlled; you cannot change that.
  • Cancelling a session booked with a package gives the session back.

Memberships ​

A membership gives you free visits to a center for a period.

  • Buy it online. It becomes active after payment and lasts for its duration, counted from activation.
  • Check in for a visit while it is active. Visits are free and confirmed immediately.
  • Each membership has a limit of visits per day.
  • You are reminded before a package or membership expires.

Where to see them ​

My packages and My memberships list yours with their status (pending, active, exhausted, expired, cancelled).
