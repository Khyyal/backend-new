Purchases and payments ​

The Purchase module records what a client buys and how it is paid. It also computes the platform fee.

Concepts ​

ConceptNotes
PurchaseOne checkout. Status pending, confirmed, cancelled, completed. Has items. Gets a unique 6-character code on insert (HasUniqueCode, same format as the reservation code).
Purchase itemtotal = unit_price × quantity − discount + tax. Its purchasable (morph alias) is a reservation, client_package, client_membership, stay_invoice (an extra charge on a stabling stay, see Reservations) or subscription.
PaymentOne attempt. Status pending, processing, succeeded, failed, cancelled, expired, refunded.
AdjustmentA promotion or coupon discount applied to a purchase. See Promotions.
PurchaseFeeThe platform fee snapshot for a center-merchant purchase.

Purchase source is client, center or system. channel is khyyal_app, center_app, khyyal_web, center_web or center_dashboard. Payment methods are online, cash_on_arrival and manual.

State changes ​

EntityAllowed transitionsOnly through
Purchasepending → confirmed, pending|confirmed → cancelled, confirmed → completedPurchaseStateService

  • Same-status transitions are a no-op.
  • succeeded is final except for refunds through recordRefund: cumulative, amount ≤ remaining, and status refunded only when fully refunded.
  • Gateways return a PaymentStatus and never write Payment.status.

Refunding a purchase from the center ​

POST centers/{center}/purchases/{purchase}/refund (also under admin/centers/{center}; idempotent; reservations.edit for center users, centers.manage_reservations for system users; the purchase must pass CenterPurchaseQuery::visible(), otherwise 404) runs Modules\Reservations\Actions\RefundPurchase in one transaction:

  1. The purchase must be confirmed (purchase_not_refundable) and have a succeeded payment with something left to refund (nothing_to_refund).
  2. The purchase is cancelled through PurchaseStateService, then everything it sold is cancelled (see Reservations).
  3. Each payment's whole remaining amount is refunded last: online payments through their gateway (a refusal or exception gives purchase_refund_failed and rolls everything back), manual payments without a gateway call. recordRefund then marks them refunded.

There is no partial refund on this endpoint. Fee reversal, redemption release, ledger sync and buyer notifications come from the existing PurchaseCancelled and PaymentRefunded listeners. Errors use ReservationNotAllowedException (422 with reason).

Payments are append-only attempts ​

A retry is a new Payment row. Never revive failed or expired payments, and never delete abandoned pending ones. A purchase is confirmed only when its succeeded payments sum to at least total_amount.

Item and payment amounts must be > 0. Manual payment is allowed only when the source is center.

Pricing order ​

line subtotals → promotions / coupon → total = subtotal − discount + tax → platform fee

The fee is what the center owes the platform. It never changes what the client pays.

  • Fee base = total_amount + sum(adjustment.amount − center_share_amount).
  • Fee = intdiv(base × bps, 10000) + fixed, raised to the minimum, cut to the maximum, capped at the base. A base ≤ 0 means a fee of 0.
  • Rule resolution picks the first active FeeRule in this order: center+channel, platform+channel, center fallback, platform fallback. There is one rule per (center, channel).
  • Write exactly one PurchaseFee per center-merchant purchase, even when it is 0. Reverse it (reversed_at) on cancel or full refund; partial refunds keep it. Never recompute or delete fee rows.
  • Only system users with fees.manage manage fee rules (admin/fee-rules).

Channel resolution ​

Client requests take the channel from the X-Channel header and may not send center_dashboard. The center dashboard uses center_dashboard. System users get null. center_app and center_web fall back to null unless the center has custom_site_enabled.

Admin and center views ​

admin/purchases and centers/{center}/purchases list and show purchases and export them. Every purchase resource includes code. centers/{center}/purchases/code/{code} (also under admin/centers/{center}/…) shows a purchase by its code with the same visibility rules as showing it by ID; clients list their own with clients/purchases (newest first, optional status) and look one up with clients/purchases/{purchase} or clients/purchases/code/{code}. Admins also get admin/purchases/{purchase}/financials. Center users see purchases according to their permissions.

admin/purchases and its export take sort (AdminPurchaseQuery::SORTS): created_at or total_amount, - for descending; newest first without it. See List sorting.
